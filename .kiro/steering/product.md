# Product: Familiar

A haunted habit tracker. The user adopts a pixel-ghost "familiar" whose life
depends on their real-world daily habits ("rituals").

## Vision

Habit trackers fail because breaking a streak costs nothing. Familiar makes
the cost emotional: your ghost visibly starves, fades, and dies if you neglect
your rituals — and the only way to revive it is a séance that severs your
streaks. Guilt, but make it cozy-spooky.

## Audience

- People who enjoy gamified productivity (Habitica, Finch, Tamagotchi nostalgia)
- Kiroween 2026 judges looking for a polished, spooky, *usable* interface

## Tone

Cozy-horror. Whimsical gothic, never gory. The app should feel like a
friendly haunting: candlelight, fog, dry humor, genuine affection for the
little ghost whose life you hold.

## Success criteria

- A judge understands the core loop within 10 seconds of seeing the screen
- The ghost feels alive (bobbing, blinking, reacting) within 3 seconds of load
- A full demo loop — onboard, feed, evolve, die, revive — takes under 2 minutes
